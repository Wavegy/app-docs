# Privacy Policy — Reflex Aim

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Advertising Information
- Advertising ID
- Device information (OS version, device model, etc.)
- IP address
- Ad impressions and clicks

### 1.2 World Ranking Data
When you submit a score to the World Ranking, the following data is
sent to and stored on our server (Supabase):
- User name (a nickname you enter, max 10 characters)
- Game score

### 1.3 Application Usage
- App settings and local scores (stored only on your device)

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- Advertising information is used to display ads through Google AdMob.
- Ranking data is used solely to display the World Ranking leaderboard.

## 3. Data Retention
- Ranking data: Only the top 10 entries per level are retained.
  Entries outside the top 10 are automatically deleted.
- App settings and local scores: Stored only on your device and
  deleted when you uninstall the app.
- Advertising data: Collected and retained by Google AdMob in
  accordance with Google's privacy policy
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Data Deletion
- You can request deletion of your World Ranking entry (user name and
  score) by contacting us via the form below. We will delete the data
  within 30 days of your request.
- Data stored locally on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
- You can reset or delete your Advertising ID from your device settings
  (Settings > Google > Ads).

## 5. Information Sharing
We do not share collected information with third parties except:
- Google AdMob for ad delivery
- Legal requirements
